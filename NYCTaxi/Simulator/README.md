Place here the auxiliary files read by `build/build_data.py` and `run.py`:

- `daily_weather_data_2009_2015.csv`, daily weather of New York City from the
  [Open-Meteo Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api)
  (columns `date`, `rain_sum`, `snowfall_sum`, `temperature_2m_max`, `temperature_2m_min`,
  `sunshine_duration`, `daylight_duration`, temperatures in degrees Fahrenheit).
- `taxi_zones/`, the NYC TLC taxi zone shapefile (`taxi_zones.dbf` and companions) from the
  [TLC trip record page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page).
